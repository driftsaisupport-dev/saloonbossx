# SaloonBossX 5.0 — what changed in the pitch

> Owners said "it's not for me". What they meant was: *"I can't picture it in
> MY salon, I'm scared of losing data, and I don't trust staff with it."* 5.0
> answers each of those on the spot.

## Before you walk in
- On your demo phone: **Settings → Demo salon** and pick the owner's type
  (parlour, unisex or men's). You get 75 days of a living salon in under a minute.
  Use **Start fresh** before you hand the phone to anyone.
- Connect your demo phone to **your own** Google Drive (Branches & Cloud), so
  you can show the backup list and the Google Sheet.
- Settings → **Language → हिन्दी** if the owner is more comfortable in Hindi.

## The new 3-minute demo (do it in this order)
1. **Home screen, Boss AI card.** *"Subah app kholte hi ye batata hai — kal
   kitna hua, kaun customer aane wala hai, kitna udhaar baaki hai, kaunsa
   staff zyada discount de raha hai."* Let them read it.
2. **Ask Boss AI** in Hinglish, out loud: *"is mahine ka profit?"*, then
   *"kaun se customer nahi aaye?"*. Tap **Send** on one row, and WhatsApp
   opens with the message already written. *"Bina internet ke, bina koi extra
   paisa."*
3. **Make a bill in 10 seconds:**
   - add a new client right in the picker;
   - tap a Quick-add service;
   - point at the AI suggestion ("41% Haircut wale Hair Spa bhi lete hain")
     and tap it;
   - choose UPI, and the QR shows the exact amount;
   - Generate.
4. **Udhaar:** *"Customer baad mein dega? Pay later — app yaad rakhega, aur
   WhatsApp pe reminder bhi bhejega."*
5. **Leakage guard:** *"Koi gadbad discount ya cancel?"*. The demo shows one
   staff member giving 2× the team's discount and cancelling bills. **This
   is the moment owners with staff decide.** Stop talking.
6. **Phone kho gaya toh?** Open Branches & Cloud → Restore from Google Drive.
   *"Roz ka backup aapke apne Google Drive mein, locked. Naya phone, 2 minute
   mein sab wapas."*
7. Only for a **chain owner**: open the HQ Dashboard. *"Sab branches ek
   screen pe — aaj ka collection, kaunsi branch peeche, kaunsa staff
   top."* Then show the Google Sheet on the same Drive.

Then open **Settings → Why SaloonBossX** and let *them* type what a monthly
app would cost them. The calculator shows the 3-year saving and the payback
in days. Ask for the sale.

1. *"₹5,999 ek baar — phir kabhi kuch nahi. Online wale ₹1,000–2,500 har mahine lete hain."*
2. If they hesitate: *"Pehle ek mahina chala ke dekho, ₹599. Pasand aaye toh ek baar wala lo — saal bhar ke monthly se sasta padta hai."*

Monthly customers are your upgrade list. In month 2–3, message them:
*"₹5,999 ek baar — 10 mahine ke monthly jitna, aur phir hamesha free."*

**Demo step 0, before billing:** open the booking link on your phone and book a slot as a customer.
The salon app gets the request. *"Aapke customer raat 11 baje bhi booking kar sakte hain —
Google Maps se, Instagram se."*

# SaloonBossX — 3-day launch plan, Navi Mumbai

> The price table below is from the 4.x launch. **Current prices are in the 5.5.1 table above**; the selling advice still applies.

## The offer (locked, do not discount below this)

| Plan | MRP | Launch price | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | ₹3,999 | **₹1,499** | Freelance makeup artist, home parlour, 1 chair |
| Standard | ₹9,999 | **₹4,999** | Normal salon: 2–8 staff, a counter, GST bills |
| Multi-Location | ₹14,999 | **₹6,999** | 2–5 branches |
| Salon + Academy | ₹19,999 | **₹9,999** | Salon running a beauty/hair academy |

Every plan: one-time payment, works offline, data stays on their phone,
7-day money-back guarantee, 99 licences at this price then it goes to MRP.

**Standard at ₹4,999 is the plan you sell.** It is the best fit for a real salon
and it is where your money is. Solo at ₹1,499 exists to catch people who say no
to ₹4,999 — never open with it.

## Be clear with yourself about the numbers

396 licences (99 × 4 plans) in 3 days is around ₹23 lakh. Nobody sells that
door-to-door in 3 days with a new app. Do not build your loan plan on it.

What is achievable by one person working hard for 3 days in Navi Mumbai:

- Visit 25–35 salons per day = 75–100 visits in 3 days
- Live demo in about half of them = 40–50 demos
- Close 10–20% of demos = **6–15 sales**
- Mostly Standard = **₹30,000 – ₹80,000 in 3 days**

With a helper doing a second area, roughly double it. That is the honest range.
Treat 99 as the month-one goal, not the 3-day goal. Beating this range means
getting a salon-products distributor or an academy to buy in bulk (below) —
that is the only realistic path to a very large number in 3 days.

## Before day 1 (do this tonight, 4–5 hours)

1. Build the APK and test it on two phones (`LAUNCH_GUIDE.md`, Part D).
2. Put `EXPO_PUBLIC_SELLER_UPI` in `.env` so the owner can pay from inside the
   app and the payment reference carries their device ID.
3. Pre-generate keys: `npm run batch -- --plan standard --count 120 --prefix NM`
   and the same for solo / multi / academy. Import the CSVs into the sheet.
4. Put the APK on your site with a short link (`bit.ly/saloonbossx`) and print
   **60 small cards**: QR code to the link, the 4 prices, your WhatsApp number.
   A local print shop does these in 2 hours for a few hundred rupees.
5. Load your own phone with **demo data**: issue yourself a key, then add a fake
   salon with 30 clients, 8 services, 3 staff, a month of bills. You will sell
   from this screen, not from an empty app.
6. Rehearse the 3-minute demo out loud 5 times. Time it.
7. Charge a power bank. You will be showing the app all day.

## The route — 3 days

Salons cluster around stations and sector markets. Work one cluster per
half-day, on foot, shop to shop.

**Day 1 (highest density, best paying)**
- Morning: Vashi — Sector 17 market, APMC side lanes, around Inorbit
- Afternoon: Nerul — Sector 19/20 market, Seawoods Darave shops
- Evening: Kharghar — Sector 12 and 35 main roads (busy till 9 pm)

**Day 2 (volume + academies)**
- Morning: CBD Belapur — Sector 11/15, and every **beauty academy** you can find
- Afternoon: Kopar Khairane — Sector 11/12 market
- Evening: Airoli — Sector 8/17 market

**Day 3 (close the pipeline, then new area)**
- Morning: follow up with everyone who said "come tomorrow" on Day 1–2. This is
  where half your sales come from.
- Afternoon: Panvel old market + Kamothe
- Evening: Ghansoli, plus final WhatsApp push to every number you collected

**Timing:** salons are dead 11 am – 1 pm and 4 – 6 pm on weekdays. That is when
the owner can talk. Avoid Saturday evening and Sunday morning — they are packed
and will throw you out. Mondays many salons are closed.

## The 3-minute demo script

Walk in, ask: *"Owner sahab/madam hain?"* Only sell to the owner. Staff cannot buy.

> "Namaste, main <name>. Main Navi Mumbai ke salons ke liye ek billing app banaya
> hai — SaloonBossX. Ek minute dijiye, dikhata hoon, aapka kaam aasaan ho jayega."

Then, on your phone, do exactly this in front of them:

1. **Make a bill** — pick 2 services, add staff, tap save. "10 second mein bill."
2. **Send it on WhatsApp** to their own number. Now the app is on their phone.
3. **Open the client's history** — "Ye customer 3 mahine mein 8 baar aayi, ₹9,400
   kharch kiya. Aapko yaad rehta hai? App yaad rakhta hai."
4. **Staff screen** — attendance and salary/commission. "Mahine ke end mein
   hisaab ka jhagda khatam."
5. **Day's report** — today's cash vs UPI. "Roz shaam ko pata chalega kitna aaya."

Then stop talking. The first person who speaks after the demo loses. Let them ask.

## Prices, and how to say them

> "Normally ₹9,999 hai. Launch offer chal raha hai — pehle 99 salons ke liye
> ₹4,999, one time. Koi monthly charge nahi. Internet ke bina bhi chalta hai.
> Aapka data aapke phone mein hi rehta hai, mere paas nahi jaata.
> Pasand na aaye toh 7 din mein paisa wapas."

Then ask for the sale directly: **"Abhi laga dun aapke phone mein?"**
Do not say "sochiye" or "kal aata hoon" yourself. Make them say it.

|---|---|

## Closing and taking money

1. Open the app on **their** phone (install it yourself from your QR card).
2. Activation screen → choose plan → **Pay by UPI now** → their UPI app opens
   with the amount and reference already filled.
3. Payment lands in your account. Take the next AVAILABLE key of that plan from
   your sheet, WhatsApp it to them, paste it in, activate.
4. **Now spend 10 minutes setting up their services and staff.** This is what
   stops refunds and creates referrals.
5. Before leaving, ask: *"Aapke jaan-pehchaan mein 2 salon hain jinko useful
   hoga? Unka number de dijiye, main aapka naam bolunga."* Write the numbers down.

Cash also works — but then send the key only after the cash is in your hand.

## Do not forget these 4 multipliers

They can beat door-to-door on their own:

1. **Beauty academies.** One academy owner is a ₹9,999 sale and they teach 30–60
   students who will open their own salons. Visit every academy in Belapur/Vashi.
2. **Salon product distributors and beauty wholesale shops** (APMC, Vashi market).
   They visit 50 salons a week. Offer ₹500–₹1,000 per sale they bring, and give
   them a stack of your cards. One good distributor = 10+ sales.
3. **WhatsApp groups of salon owners.** Every area has one. Ask each buyer to add
   you or forward one message with the offer, price and link.
4. **Instagram / local Facebook groups** ("Navi Mumbai Salon Owners", area
   groups). Post the 30-second demo video 3 times a day for 3 days. Video is the
   ad — show a bill being made and sent on WhatsApp, nothing else.

If Day 1 ends with 0 sales: your demo is too long, or you are pitching staff
instead of owners, or you are not asking for the sale. Fix that before Day 2 —
do not lower the price.

## After the 3 days

Every "no" is a list, not a loss. Message all of them on day 7:
*"Sir, launch price ₹4,999 kal khatam ho raha hai, uske baad ₹9,999. Chahiye toh
bata dijiye."* Deadlines close more sales than discounts do.

Keep the ₹1,499 Solo plan running permanently — home parlours and freelancers are
a much bigger market in Navi Mumbai than salons, and they buy faster.
