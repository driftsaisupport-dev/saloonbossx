STEP 1

Install the app

  1. Open the download link your seller sent on WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the downloaded file SaloonBossX.apk.
  3. If Android says "For your security…", tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back → Install.
  4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. The app is new and not yet in the Play Store; that is why Android asks.

Use the phone that stays at the counter. One licence works on one phone (Chain: 3, Academy: 2).

STEP 2

First setup: 5 short questions

  1. Language: English, हिंदी, मराठी and more. You can change it later.
  2. Business type: Salon, Beauty parlour, Unisex, Spa… A ready menu with typical prices loads.
  3. Your salon: name, owner name, phone, city. These print on every bill.
  4. Services: untick what you don't do. Prices can be changed later.
  5. Team: add staff names. Keep I also serve clients myself on if you work too.

Keep Free cloud backup switched on. It turns itself on as soon as there is internet, and protects your data if the phone is lost.

Already using SaloonBossX on another phone? Switch the cloud backup off here and follow Step 10 instead.

STEP 4

Finish the "Set up your salon" checklist

  • Salon details & logo: Settings → Salon Profile. Add the address and a logo for bills.
  • UPI ID: customers scan a QR with the exact amount on the bill screen.
  • Cloud backup: Turn on if it isn't ticked yet.
  • Save your recovery key: Save → send it to yourself on WhatsApp (see Step 10).
  • Google review link: see Step 6.
  • Start online booking: Turn on, one tap (Step 7).

STEP 5

Make your first bill (about 10 seconds)

  1. Tap New Bill on the home screen.
  2. Pick the customer, or add a new one with just name and phone. Walk-in is fine too.
  3. Tap services from the quick list. Boss AI suggests add-ons customers usually take together.
  4. Choose payment: Cash, UPI (QR shows the exact amount), Card, Split or Pay later (udhaar).
  5. Tap Save → send the bill on WhatsApp, as a PDF, or print on a 58/80 mm printer.

Bluetooth thermal printer? Install the free RawBT app once, pair the printer, then choose the size in Settings → Bill design & printer.

STEP 6

Get more Google reviews automatically

  1. On your phone, open Google Maps while signed in to the Google account that owns your business profile.
  2. Open your salon → Ask for reviews (or "Get more reviews" / "Share review form") → Copy link.
  3. In SaloonBossX: Settings → Google reviews → Paste.

From now on every WhatsApp bill asks for a review, printed and PDF bills carry a review QR, and your booking page shows a ★ Google reviews button.

No Google Business Profile yet? Create one free at business.google.com. It is the main way new customers find salons nearby.

STEP 7

Online booking with your own short link

  1. Settings → Online Booking → Start online booking — 1 tap. Wait 10–20 seconds.
  2. You get a link with your salon's name, like …/glow-beauty-vashi. Tap Test; it should say Working ✅.
  3. Tap Share and put the link in:
    • Google Business Profile → Edit profile → Bookings link (adds a "Book" button on Google Maps)
    • Instagram → Edit profile → Links
    • WhatsApp Business → Business profile → Website
  4. Print the QR poster for the counter, so walk-ins book their next visit before leaving.

When a customer books: you get a notification. Open Online Booking → Accept. It goes into your calendar and WhatsApp opens with a confirmation ready to send. Or tap Other time / Decline.

Opening hours and the weekly off day are set for you. Change them in Setup & link → Save & publish.

STEP 8

Bring in old customers and give staff their own login

  • Customers: Clients → import button → From phone contacts (tick who is a customer) or From Excel/CSV (columns Name, Phone, Birthday). Duplicates are skipped.
  • Staff logins: Settings → Users & Access → add each staff member with a 4-digit PIN. Staff don't see your income unless you allow it.
  • Commission: Staff → set commission % and salary type. Pay slips are calculated from their bills and attendance.
STEP 9

  • Regulars who are due for their next visit
  • Customers who stopped coming (with how much they used to spend)
  • Birthdays this week · your quiet weekday · the next festival
  • Recent customers to ask for a Google review

Pick one → check the message (English or Hinglish) → tap Send to Priya (1/23). WhatsApp opens with her name filled in; tap send, come back, and the next customer is ready. It is free and personal.

Send up to about 50 a day from your own WhatsApp. Photo & video lets you post your work with a ready caption and hashtags; Poster studio makes offer posters with your photo.

STEP 10

Save your recovery key (do this today)

The recovery key opens your encrypted cloud backup. Without it, a lost phone means lost data.

  1. Home checklist → Save, or Settings → Branches & Cloud → Save recovery key.
  2. Send it to yourself on WhatsApp ("Message yourself") and keep that chat.

New or lost phone: install the app → activate your licence → Settings → Branches & Cloud → Join / new phone → paste the recovery key → turn on This is the owner's new phone → pick the latest backup → Restore. Everything is back in about 2 minutes.

Share the recovery key with nobody except your own branch phones.

STEP 11

More than one salon (Chain plan)

  1. On the owner's phone: Branches & Cloud → Add a branch phone → send the link on WhatsApp.
  2. On the branch phone: install, activate with the same Chain key, tap the link → Open in SaloonBossX → name the branch → Join chain.
  3. On the owner's phone: HQ Dashboard shows today, this month, staff, stock and alerts for every branch. Pull down to refresh.
  4. To change prices everywhere: set them on the owner's phone → Publish price list to all branches.

Branches keep working without internet. Their figures reach the owner's phone the next time they are online.

STEP 12

Monthly plan: renewing

  • 7 days before the end date, a reminder appears on the home screen.
  • Pay your seller → paste the new key in Settings → License & Plan → Renew / extend. The new month is added after your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing.
  • Paid by UPI and the seller renewed it for you? Tap I have already paid — check again.
  • After the end date you still have 3 days. Nothing is ever deleted.

One-time costs less than a year of monthly payments. Switch any time from License & Plan with a one-time key; your data stays as it is.
