# SaloonBossX market review (October 2026)

Written from the point of view of an owner running ten kinds of salon business. Competitor facts were checked on live pages on 12–13 October 2026; links are at the end. Prices change, so re-check before quoting them to a customer.

## 1. The market in one paragraph
Almost every competitor is cloud-only and charged every month or year. Indian tools (Zenoti, DINGG, Salonist, MioSalon, Zylu, Easy Salon, GlamPlus, Salon360, SalonBoost, TapGro) mostly hide prices behind demos; published or third-party figures run from about ₹800 to ₹15,000+ a month. Global tools (Fresha, Booksy, Vagaro, Square, Phorest, Mangomint, Timely, GlossGenius, Shortcuts, Pabau) charge in dollars, add per-staff, per-message or new-client fees, and several don't operate in India at all. Owners' top complaints are: needs internet, hidden and rising fees, poor support after the sale, crashes, too many taps at the counter, unreliable WhatsApp reminders, wrong commission reports and data lock-in. No competitor we checked has an academy module.

## 4. Where SaloonBossX wins
1. **Works with no internet**: billing, clients, reports, queue and day close all run offline. Most competitors stop working.
2. **One-time price** from ₹1,499: pays for itself in about 2–6 months against monthly rivals. Only Shortcuts (Australia) offers anything similar.
3. **No per-message or new-client fees**: WhatsApp bills and reminders go from the owner's own WhatsApp.
4. **Academy module** no competitor shows.
5. **Owner's data stays on their phone**, encrypted, with backups they control, so there's no lock-in.
6. Built for India: GST 5% with CGST/SGST, Indian financial year, UPI, udhaar/dues, prepaid wallets, Hindi and Marathi.

## 5. Honest gaps (roadmap)
- Android only today (iOS build not yet published).
- WhatsApp sending is one tap per message, not fully automatic (the trade-off for zero message fees).
- No online deposit payment for bookings (the advance is recorded when the customer pays by UPI).
- Spa rooms/beds as bookable resources, signed digital consent forms, stock transfer between branches, PF/ESI payroll.
- Not yet tested on a wide range of real phones: run the device test checklist in README before every release.
